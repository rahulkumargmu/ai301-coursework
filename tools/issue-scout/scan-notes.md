# issue-scout scan notes

- 2026-10-14 (strategy, before scan 1): I tried the queries in the terminal before trusting the scout with them. Under the default order, `--limit 100` returned only issues opened in the last few days, and more than half were filed by the owner of a brand-new personal repo (52 of 100 were OWNER). Changed: added `--sort updated` and `--created "<2026-09-23"` to every query, plus a ranking rule that drops `authorAssociation == OWNER`, plus one issue per repo. I kept `OR` out of the queries because `gh search issues` wraps the words after `--` in parentheses, which changed the results in the activity.
- 2026-10-14 (scan 1, $1.02): 293 combined results, 69 after ranking, 2 accepted (orchestrator-core#1872, Lespass#450) and 3 rejected, all on scope-fits (aeon-neuro#78 umbrella checklist, open-nvr#29 empty body, cluv#200 fix approach unsettled). I checked all three rejects by reading the issues myself and they're correct.
- 2026-10-14 (rubric fix, ai-policy): Both accepts said "no CONTRIBUTING.md in root or .github/, silent policy". That's wrong for both repos. orchestrator-core keeps its guidelines in `docs/contributing/guidelines.md` along with a `CLAUDE.md` (AI-friendly), and Lespass has `GUIDELINES.md` at the root. Neither bans AI, so the verdicts stood, but a ban kept in `docs/` would have been missed. Changed: in live mode the ai-policy evidence now comes from the full repo tree (CONTRIBUTING*, GUIDELINES*, docs/**/contribut*, AI_POLICY*, AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, PR template), and the evidence line has to name the files read. Eval-mode evidence is unchanged.
- 2026-10-14 (strategy fix, ranking): 2 of the 3 rejects were visible in the issue `body`, a field gh can return for free, so paying to grade them was waste. Changed: added `body` to the `--json` fields, plus rule 6a (drop bodies under 80 characters) and rule 6b (drop bodies with 6+ task boxes). The cluv#200 kind of reject (approach unsettled) still needs the rubric.
- 2026-10-14 (fit, not a fix): Lespass#450 passed every check, but the project works in French (issue text, comments, and i18n source strings). No free result field can tell what language an issue is written in, so this stays a reading call when I choose.
- 2026-10-14 (checking the fix without a paid re-scan): Re-ran the three queries with `body` and applied the updated rules by hand with jq. Rule 6a drops open-nvr#29 and rule 6b drops aeon-neuro#78, each among 5 results it removes. The new top five is orchestrator-core#1872, Lespass#450, cluv#200, tapio#137, and agents-shipgate#593, so the two slots that went to waste now go to issues the rubric hasn't seen. I didn't run a second paid scan.
